Saxophone & clarinet
I accept students of all ages, from beginners to adults. Lessons include fundamentals/scales as well as level-appropriate repertoire.
Music theory
I teach analysis techniques for music of all styles, from the Baroque to post-tonal. I also coach students in aural skills (sight singing and dictation) for exams including AP music theory.
Formerly of Westminster Conservatory? I taught saxophone and clarinet at Westminster Conservatory from 2014 to 2022, before its band and orchestral instrument programs were discontinued. While the conservatory is no longer in Princeton, my home studio is still available and located close by in Kingston off of Academy St.
